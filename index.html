FAQ

What is Ink mfers?

A 4,444-piece doodle collection inspired by the mfers style, minting on the Ink chain. It's built for people who like the art and the vibe, not for a promised payoff.

Is there a roadmap?

No. There's no roadmap, no promises, and no guarantees. What you see is what you get: a picture of a mfer doing nothing in particular.

Which chain is this on?

Ink — pun fully intended.

How do I mint one?

Head to the collection page on OpenSea, connect a wallet, and mint during whichever phase you're eligible for.
